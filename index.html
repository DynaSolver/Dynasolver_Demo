Data notes

  • Source. Articulated Robot CFD Data Generation, solved with the WaterLily dense-grid solver. The robot follows a prescribed motion and acts as a passive rigid body; no two-way fluid-structure coupling is modelled.
  • Wind cases. Two of 16 conditions are drawn per robot: speed u = 2-8 m/s, yaw -20 to +20 degrees. The two clips of one robot show the same motion under two different winds.
  • Numerics. Grid 150x50x100, 200 solver steps, exported at 900x600, 10 fps, 50 frames. The t burned into a frame is that case's own simulation time, so clips do not share a common clock.
  • Accuracy section. Case 1 is the quadruped a1 at u = 4 m/s, yaw 0 degrees; case 2 is the humanoid h1_2 at u = 2 m/s, yaw -20 degrees. Frames 6-24 are shown.
  • The reference panel shows the simulated field itself and is recolored every frame. The three prediction panels show the pointwise deviation from that field on the robot surface and along the material pathlines, on one shared per-case scale, so they can be compared directly.
  • Table values are the median pointwise surface error averaged over the 19 frames shown. Lower is better.
