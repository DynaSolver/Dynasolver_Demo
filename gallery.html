Data notes

  • Video gallery. Source: Articulated Robot CFD Data Generation — solved with the WaterLily dense-grid solver. The robot follows a prescribed motion and acts as a passive rigid body; no two-way fluid–structure coupling is modelled.
  • Two wind cases are drawn for each robot from a pool of 16: wind speed u = 2–8 m/s, yaw angle −20° … +20°. The two clips of one robot show the same motion under two different wind conditions.
  • Simulation grid 150×50×100, 200 output steps; exported clips are 900×600 · 10 fps · 50 frames (one frame per solver output).
  • The t value burned into each frame is that case's own simulation time, and the time step differs between robots, so clips do not share a common start or end time.
  • Accuracy comparison. Case 1 is the quadruped a1 at u = 4 m/s, yaw 0°; case 2 is the humanoid h1_2 at u = 2 m/s, yaw −20°. Frames 6–24 of each rollout are shown.
  • The reference panel shows the simulated field itself. The three prediction panels show the pointwise deviation from that field, rendered on the robot surface and along the material pathlines, using one shared color scale per case and quantity — because the scales are identical, the panels can be compared directly.
  • Table values are the per-frame median pointwise surface error reported by the visualization pipeline, averaged over the 19 displayed frames. They describe surface accuracy only and are not a substitute for the full benchmark.
  • Selection seed: 20260925. Interaction: hover to play, the two clips of a robot can be synced, and clicking any frame opens the side-by-side compare view (← → switch robot, space play/pause, [ ] step frames).
